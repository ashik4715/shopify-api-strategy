## 1. Executive Summary

* **The Problem:** You want to sell products with Shopify and need a real storefront live fast, without spending the full budget before your first sale.
* **The Solution:** A WordPress site driven by the Shopify plugin (ShopWP) — Shopify owns products, cart and checkout; WordPress owns pages, blog and SEO.
* **The Goal:** Store live in **4 weeks** at a fixed **৳58,000**, with 15 products live and a COD + bKash flow for the Bangladeshi market.
* **The Honest Trade-off:** Lowest price today — but **maintenance-heavy**: two bills and two update cycles (WordPress + Shopify) forever, a 75–85 Lighthouse speed ceiling, and buy-buttons that are never 100% native. If you plan to run this store for years, see **Option B** on the following proposal.

## 2. Project Scope & Deliverables

Delivery is organized in three tight, sequential phases:

* **Phase 1 — Foundation & Design (Days 1–7):** Hosting, domain, SSL, WordPress install, Shopify store configuration, Figma design of 6 templates (home, collection, PDP, lookbook, cart, contact).
* **Phase 2 — Build & Integration (Days 8–21):** Custom Gutenberg theme, ShopWP buy-buttons styled native, cart drawer sync, payments (COD, manual bKash/Nagad, SSLCommerz-ready), 15 products uploaded with variants.
* **Phase 3 — Growth Kit, QA & Launch (Days 22–28):** Reviews app, FB Pixel, GA4, sitemap + schema, cross-device QA, one consolidated revision round, training videos and handover.

**Included in the quote:** Up to 15 products with variants · 5 collections · lookbook/drop storytelling sections · cart drawer + Shopify secure checkout · COD + bKash/Nagad manual · reviews, IG feed, blog, search · Pixel + GA4 + basic SEO · ONE revision round + 14-day bug-fix window.
**Explicitly excluded:** Logo/brand identity design · product photography · paid app subscriptions · multilingual store · custom checkout UI (Shopify Plus only).
